Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

📋 Overview

GlobeVault is a geography and culture quiz game developed by Kadesh. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. The short version: we collect nothing from you.

📱 Data Stored on Your Device

GlobeVault stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your quiz progress (which nations you have completed)
  • Your best scores and star ratings per nation
  • Your music and sound preferences

This data never leaves your device and is used solely to save your progress between sessions. You can clear it at any time from your device's app settings.

🌐 Internet Access

GlobeVault does not require an internet connection to play. The app does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or databases. No data is ever transmitted from your device.

👶 Children's Privacy

GlobeVault is a family-friendly educational quiz game suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it is completely safe for children to use.

🔗 Third-Party Services

GlobeVault does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, social media plugins, or tracking libraries. There are no in-app advertisements of any kind.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is ever updated, the revised version will be available within the app's Play Store listing. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

✉️ Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you are welcome to reach out via the contact information on the Google Play Store listing for GlobeVault.
